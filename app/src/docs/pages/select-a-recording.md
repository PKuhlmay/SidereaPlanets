# Select a recording

Press **Select** in the step *Open* (or in the toolbar) and pick a recording. SidereaPlanets reads it frame by frame, so even long videos with thousands of frames do not have to fit into memory.

## Supported files

| Format | Extensions | What works |
|---|---|---|
| SER | .ser | 8 to 16 bit, mono and colour |
| AVI video | .avi | Uncompressed, Lagarith, MJPEG, HuffYUV |
| Still frames | .bmp .jpg .tif .png .fit | Not available yet |

> Your recording is only read, never changed.

## Step by step

1. Press **Select** at the bottom of the step panel, or at the top left of the toolbar. Both do the same. The panel also lists the files you opened last under *Recent*; a click opens one of them.
2. Choose a .ser or .avi file. The file type list in the dialog is the same as in the reference program.
3. The recording opens and the step *Alignpoints* follows. Name, codec, size and frame count appear at the right of the step bar.

To open another recording later, click the step *Open* again, or press **Select** in the toolbar. A new recording starts the work over.

More details on the files are on the page [Supported formats](supported-formats).
