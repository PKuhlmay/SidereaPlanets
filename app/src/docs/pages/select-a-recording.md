# Select a recording

Press **Select** in the toolbar and pick a recording. SidereaPlanets reads it frame by frame, so even long videos with thousands of frames do not have to fit into memory.

## Supported files

| Format | Extensions | What works |
|---|---|---|
| SER | .ser | 8 to 16 bit, mono and colour |
| AVI video | .avi | Uncompressed, Lagarith, MJPEG, HuffYUV |
| Still frames | .bmp .jpg .tif .png .fit | Not available yet |

> Your recording is only read, never changed.

## Step by step

1. Press **Select** at the top left of the toolbar.
2. Choose a .ser or .avi file. The file type list in the dialog is the same as in the reference program.
3. The recording opens on the Align tab. Name, codec, size and frame count appear at the right of the tab row.

More details on the files are on the page [Supported formats](supported-formats).
