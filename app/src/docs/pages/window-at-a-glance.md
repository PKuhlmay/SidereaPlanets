# The window at a glance

The window has the same parts as the reference program, from top to bottom:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Toolbar | **Select** opens a recording. The menus MRU, Flat/Dark/Reference, Tools and Settings sit next to it, **Cancel** and **Pause** in the middle, **Help**, **About** and the number of CPUs on the right |
| Tabs | Align, Stack and Wavelet as numbered steps. A step opens once the one before it is done |
| Action row | The buttons for the current step and its options |
| Work area | Settings on the left, the image in the middle |
| Status bar | Progress, messages, position and time |

> The window is being built step by step. Parts that do not work yet are marked **Not available yet** on the page that describes them.

## Help

**Help** in the toolbar opens this documentation. Press it again, or press Escape, to go back to your work. Your recording and settings stay as they were.

## Light and dark

The window is dark by default, so it does not tint how you judge the colours of an image. A light theme is planned: **Not available yet**.
